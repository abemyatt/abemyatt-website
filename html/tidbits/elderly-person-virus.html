Elderly Person Phone Virus Nightmare – 19/05/2026

Yesterday, my elderly neighbour knocked my door with his Wife's phone in his hand. "It's got a virus. Can I leave it with you?"

I had a look and saw at some point they installed an 'in development' app from the google play store called 'messages'. This app installed a custom launcher apk onto the phone that took over the home screen and redirected all apps and searches and asked for permission to do all sorts of things on their phone.

I managed to remove this app and went to their house to talk through what happened and how to avoid it happening again. They told me "We asked ChatGPT to help but it was saying too many different things and it was too complicated so we couldn't fix it... "

It's interesting to see how AI is being used by people of all ages to solve issues they cannot fix themselves. It's also interesting to see how easy it is for malware to be installed on any device. If you suspect you have a virus or malware, one of the most effective things to do is disconnect the device from the internet, factory reset the device, scan the network for malware, and if needed reset other devices.

Main points:

  • Don't download 'in development' apps from the playstore.
  • The playstore does not protect you from viruses, even if you scan with Play Protect.
  • Not every virus is detectable by anti-virus or malware scanning tools.
  • ChatGPT can't solve everything for everyone, and experience and expertise is still useful.
